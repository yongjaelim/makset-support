PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Workout records are kept on your device.
Here is how the app, Watch, support and website handle information.

Effective · November 16, 2026

At a glance

This policy applies to the MAKSET app and support website. For privacy questions, complaints or requests, contact MAKSET support at libertasab@gmail.com.

Workout logging works without registration and does not automatically send workout journals to a developer server. The optional community supports nickname posting and supported provider sign-in. There are no ads, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Local workout records and public community content have separate data flows.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps the following in its storage on your device:

  • Workout dates, exercises and custom exercises, sets, weights, reps, completion states, notes and workout history
  • Saved routines, the active workout and rest deadlines
  • Preferences such as language, weight units, alerts and weekly goals

This information is used to record, display and analyze workouts and restore an active workout. The app uses iPhone operating-system app isolation and file protection. The app does not read or write HealthKit data.

Your paired Apple Watch

The companion app exchanges the active workout’s name, start time, exercises, sets, weights, reps, completion states, rest timer, language and units with your paired Watch. Changes made on Watch are saved on iPhone.

Past workout history, notes and routine lists are not sent to Watch. Communication uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity, and the operating system may manage associated transmission state. No developer server is added for this feature.

Optional rest alerts

Notification permission is requested only when you enable Rest alerts in the app. Alerts are scheduled locally and contain no workout records or notes.

You can turn notifications off in the app or iPhone Settings. Turning Rest alerts off in the app cancels scheduled rest alerts. Focus, silent mode and other operating-system settings can affect delivery.

Retention, deletion and device backups

You can delete workout records and routines in the app. Custom exercise definitions and recovery copies of damaged storage files may remain separately.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Offload App on iPhone keeps data, so it differs from deleting the app. Device backups are managed separately through operating-system settings; restoring a backup may restore older app data.

MAKSET does not provide its own cloud sync or in-app file export/import. The developer cannot remotely access or restore your local records.

Optional community and sign-in

The community is a public board for training experiences and questions. Reading does not require an account. The MAKSET server in South Korea stores nicknames, posts, comments, selected photos, timestamps, likes, reports, blocks and author identifiers to provide and moderate the community. Posts, nicknames, comments and approved photos are public. Private workout journals, routines and history are not automatically attached or uploaded.

Anonymous authors use a random author ID and a secret token stored in the iPhone Keychain. Sign-in is optional; Apple, Google, Naver and Kakao become available as their setup is completed. MAKSET does not collect provider passwords. The server stores a hash of the provider identifier, account links and encrypted credentials needed to revoke the link. Names, emails and profile photos returned during sign-in are not stored as MAKSET account details. You may consent to linking this device’s anonymous activity to your account.

Photos are uploaded when you submit a post. Original metadata, including location, is removed and images are resized. Until approval, only the author and moderators can view them. Do not post unnecessary personal information or unauthorized photos. Moderators can review reports and pending photos, hide content and restrict authors.

Delete your own posts and comments through their actions, or delete anonymous author information or your signed-in account and its content from the community account screen. If provider unlinking fails, deletion is not marked complete and can be retried. User deletion clears stored text, nicknames and attached photos from operational data; it cannot recall copies others have saved or cached on other devices. Moderator hiding differs from deletion and may retain content for review. Operators remove retained material when no longer needed.

Anonymous management rights depend on the Keychain identity. Changing devices or losing it may prevent management of older posts. Removing the app alone does not delete server content. Contact support for deletion or access requests; verification may be needed.

Connection IPs are processed temporarily in server memory for request limits and security. API URLs, authentication tokens and post bodies are not written to access logs. OAuth communicates with your selected provider over HTTPS and that provider’s policy also applies. Data is not used for ads or cross-app/site tracking.

Voluntary support correspondence

The email address, message and attachments you choose to send are used to handle your inquiry. The app does not automatically attach workout records to emails.

Google Gmail is used to deliver and store support correspondence. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s service processing and international processing practices.

Support information is retained as needed to respond and follow up, then deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is legally required. Send access, correction or deletion requests to the support email. Verification may be needed to process a request.

This website

This is a static website for support information and the privacy policy. We have not added analytics tools, ads, tracking cookies or a contact form to its code. Language switching uses links to the corresponding translated page.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. For security, GitHub records and retains visitor IP addresses, including visits by people who are not signed in. Consult GitHub’s documentation and privacy statement for information handled by the hosting service and its retention practices.

Changes and contact

If app features or information handling change, we will update this policy and show its effective date. Send privacy questions and requests to libertasab@gmail.com.
