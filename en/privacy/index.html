PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Workout records are kept on your device.
Here is how the app, Watch, support and website handle information.

Effective · November 16, 2026

At a glance

This policy applies to the MAKSET app and support website. For privacy questions, complaints or requests, contact MAKSET support at libertasab@gmail.com.

Workout logging works without registration and does not automatically send workout journals to a developer server. The optional community supports nickname posting and supported provider sign-in. There are no ads, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Local workout records and public community content have separate data flows.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps the following in its storage on your device:

  • Workout dates, exercises and custom exercises, sets, weights, reps, completion states, notes and workout history
  • Saved routines, the active workout and rest deadlines
  • Preferences such as language, weight units, alerts and weekly goals

This information is used to record, display and analyze workouts and restore an active workout. The app uses iPhone operating-system app isolation and file protection. The app does not read or write HealthKit data.

Your paired Apple Watch

The companion app exchanges the active workout’s name, start time, exercises, sets, weights, reps, completion states, rest timer, language and units with your paired Watch. Changes made on Watch are saved on iPhone.

Past workout history, notes and routine lists are not sent to Watch. Communication uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity, and the operating system may manage associated transmission state. No developer server is added for this feature.

Optional rest alerts

Notification permission is requested only when you enable Rest alerts in the app. Alerts are scheduled locally and contain no workout records or notes.

You can turn notifications off in the app or iPhone Settings. Turning Rest alerts off in the app cancels scheduled rest alerts. Focus, silent mode and other operating-system settings can affect delivery.

Retention, deletion and device backups

You can delete workout records and routines in the app. Custom exercise definitions and recovery copies of damaged storage files may remain separately.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Offload App on iPhone keeps data, so it differs from deleting the app. Device backups are managed separately through operating-system settings; restoring a backup may restore older app data.

MAKSET does not provide its own cloud sync or in-app file export/import. The developer cannot remotely access or restore your local records.

Optional community and sign-in

The community is a public board for training experiences and questions. Reading does not require an account. The MAKSET server in South Korea stores nicknames, posts, comments, selected photos, timestamps, likes, reports, blocks and author identifiers to provide and moderate the community. Posts, nicknames, comments and approved photos are public. Private workout journals, routines and history are not automatically attached or uploaded.

Anonymous authors use a random author ID and a secret token stored in the iPhone Keychain. Sign-in is optional; Apple, Google, Naver and Kakao become available as their setup is completed. MAKSET does not collect provider passwords. The server stores a hash of the provider identifier, account links and encrypted credentials needed to revoke the link. Names, emails and profile photos returned during sign-in are not stored as MAKSET account details. You may consent to linking this device’s anonymous activity to your account.

Photos are uploaded when you submit a post. Original metadata, including location, is removed and images are resized. Until approval, only the author and moderators can view them. Do not post unnecessary personal information or unauthorized photos. Moderators can review reports and pending photos, hide content and restrict authors.

Delete your own posts and comments through their actions, or delete anonymous author information or your signed-in account and its content from the community account screen. If provider unlinking fails, deletion is not marked complete and can be retried. User deletion clears stored text, nicknames and attached photos from operational data; it cannot recall copies others have saved or cached on other devices. Moderator hiding differs from deletion and may retain content for review. Operators remove retained material when no longer needed.

Anonymous management rights depend on the Keychain identity. Changing devices or losing it may prevent management of older posts. Removing the app alone does not delete server content. Contact support for deletion or access requests; verification may be needed.

Connection IPs are processed temporarily in server memory for request limits and security. API URLs, authentication tokens and post bodies are not written to access logs. OAuth communicates with your selected provider over HTTPS and that provider’s policy also applies. Data is not used for ads or cross-app/site tracking.

Purposes, legal basis and optional features

Random author IDs, nicknames, posts, comments, selected photos, account links and the accepted Terms version, confirmation time and age-14+ declaration are processed to provide the community requested by the user and manage accounts and permissions. The baseline is processing necessary to form or perform the service contract under Article 15(1)(4) of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act. Reporting, blocking and abuse prevention are limited to safe operation and protection of rights, applying relevant legitimate-interest or legal-obligation grounds as appropriate.

Workout journals and routines stay on the device and are not automatically combined with a server account. Photos and provider sign-in are optional; local logging and anonymous posting do not require those features. Publishing or reading this Privacy Policy is not blanket consent to future ads, marketing, disclosure or new collection. Processing requiring separate consent will have its items, purposes, retention and refusal effects explained separately.

Children under 14 and sensitive information

Community is restricted to ages 14+. Before first use, users confirm that they are at least 14 and, if minors, have their legal guardian’s permission. The app does not verify age through dates of birth or ID documents. If you do not meet this requirement, do not use Community. If information from an author under 14 is identified, support takes necessary verification, restriction and deletion steps.

Do not publish diagnoses, medical history, government identifiers or other unnecessary sensitive information. Faces, tattoos and photo backgrounds may identify a person. You may avoid publication and use the local workout journal instead. Remove accidentally published information through the app or request assistance from support.

Retention and deletion by category

Published content, nicknames, photos and author-management information are retained to provide those functions, with necessary deletion when you delete content/identity or its purpose ends. The Terms version, confirmation time and age declaration establish the conditions of author/account use and are deleted with the relevant identity/account. A separate small record in the app’s community storage remembers local acceptance.

Signed-in sessions are valid for 30 days. Login attempts last up to 10 minutes; one-time app handoff codes last up to 90 seconds. Expired items cannot authenticate and are removed by periodic maintenance. Uploaded photos not attached to a post become eligible for cleanup after 24 hours; recent uploads can be retained to support retries.

Provider tokens needed for unlinking are encrypted on the server and removed on account deletion or unlinking. A verified provider unlink notification ends relevant sessions and removes that link; the Community account and its content are erased when it was the last sign-in link. Accounts with another link remain. Hashed identifiers preventing in-flight revoked sign-ins from recreating an account become eligible for cleanup after 24 hours; hashed notification IDs used for duplicate handling after 31 days. Periodic maintenance removes them. Raw notifications, emails and original provider identifiers are not stored.

Reports and restriction records are retained only as needed for the case, rights and appeals; the operator reviews and removes them when that purpose ends. Support mail is deleted when the query and follow-up no longer need it. Any legally required preservation is managed separately by its legal basis, items and period. There are currently no payment/e-commerce records or automatic external backups.

Electronic deletion removes text, nicknames and photos from operational storage. Minimal row identifiers for retry/deletion state can remain; independent comments or reports by others have their own purposes and rights. Removing the app affects device data and is not a server-content deletion request.

Access, correction, deletion and other rights

The privacy and complaint contact is MAKSET support at libertasab@gmail.com, handled directly by the individual operator. It is not a separate corporation or outsourced support center. Exercise applicable rights, including access, correction, deletion, suspension and withdrawal, through app controls or support email. Only minimal information needed to verify account/author rights may be requested; complete ID copies or unnecessary workout/medical records are not routinely required.

Delete your own posts/comments or account/anonymous identity through the app. Request other corrections from support, or delete and repost the content. Applicable legal deadlines and procedures are followed; grounds and review methods are provided when a request must be limited or refused. Refusal of new Terms does not remove statutory rights over existing data. Korea’s Privacy Portal and Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee also provide official assistance.

External services and international processing

Community databases and photos are stored on the operator’s server in South Korea; local workout journals are not automatically uploaded. Optional sign-in is available through Apple, Google, Naver and Kakao. Their sign-in pages are operated by the respective providers. MAKSET retains a one-way hash of each provider-scoped identifier for account linking, discarding returned names, emails and profile pictures.

The selected provider receives sign-in requests, one-time authorization codes, issued tokens and token refresh/unlink requests over HTTPS. Recipients are Apple Inc. (United States, apple.com/privacy/contact), Google LLC (United States, policies.google.com/privacy), NAVER Corporation (South Korea, privacy.naver.com) and Kakao Corp. (South Korea, www.kakao.com/policy/privacy). These are company locations, not guarantees of storage location. Apple and Google may process authentication in the United States and other countries described in their policies. Their own account data and retention follow those policies. No provider sign-in request is sent unless you choose that provider. Refusing social sign-in leaves local logging and anonymous Community available.

Voluntary support email is received and stored using Google Gmail. Your sender address, subject, message and chosen attachments reach Gmail when you send email. Google may use processing facilities in the United States and other countries; this service does not select a mail storage region. The operator uses it for support and removes mail no longer needed. Refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for its processing. GitHub Pages processing for the public website is explained in the website section below.

Voluntary support correspondence

The email address, message and attachments you choose to send are used to handle your inquiry. The app does not automatically attach workout records to emails.

Google Gmail is used to deliver and store support correspondence. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s service processing and international processing practices.

Support information is retained as needed to respond and follow up, then deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is legally required. Send access, correction or deletion requests to the support email. Verification may be needed to process a request.

This website

This is a static website for support information and the privacy policy. We have not added analytics tools, ads, tracking cookies or a contact form to its code. Language switching uses links to the corresponding translated page.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. For security, GitHub records and retains visitor IP addresses, including visits by people who are not signed in. Consult GitHub’s documentation and privacy statement for information handled by the hosting service and its retention practices.
