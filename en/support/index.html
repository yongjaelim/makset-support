Workout logging

How do I start a workout?

Start a free workout from the Workout screen or choose a saved routine. Add an exercise, enter the weight and reps, then mark the set complete. Completed sets are included in your saved workout and statistics.

Can I add an exercise that is missing?

Choose Create an exercise in the exercise picker. Enter a name, muscle group and optional equipment, then save it for reuse. The name and equipment you enter stay as written when you change the app language.

What if different machines use kg and lbs?

Choose kg or lbs for each exercise on the workout screen. The unit selected in Settings is used for overall workout volume and record displays. Changing the unit preserves the actual weight of existing records.

Which sets are saved when I finish?

Only sets marked complete are included in the finished workout record. Before finishing, check the completion marks and the number of sets to be saved. If an input issue appears, correct the weight or reps before continuing.

What does weekly analysis compare?

The History screen compares actual squat, bench press and deadlift records, plus total workout duration, for the selected week and the previous week. It does not provide estimated 1RM or a ranking against other users. Missing records are shown as missing, rather than invented comparisons.

Apple Watch

What can I do on Watch?

Edit weight, reps, set completion and the rest timer for a workout started on iPhone. Start, finish or discard workouts and add exercises on iPhone. Your iPhone holds the authoritative workout records.

What happens when Watch disconnects?

You can refer to the displayed information, but changes are restricted while disconnected. Offline edit requests are not queued. Check the connection between your devices and open both apps to reconnect.

Are workouts written to the Health app?

MAKSET does not currently use HealthKit to save workouts or read Health app data. The Watch app is a companion for MAKSET workout logging.

Records and settings

Can I resume after reopening the app?

A successfully saved active workout can be reopened and continued. If an unsaved-input indicator appears, check your entries. Reopening the app is different from deleting and reinstalling it, which removes local app data.

Can records be restored after switching devices or deleting the app?

There is no built-in cloud sync or file export/import. Records are kept in the app’s storage on your device. Whether operating-system backups include the app’s data depends on device settings. The developer cannot remotely access or restore your local records.

Why am I not receiving rest alerts?

Enable Rest alerts in the app and check that notifications for MAKSET are allowed in iPhone Settings. Focus, silent mode and device settings can affect delivery. Turning alerts off cancels scheduled rest alerts.

How do I change the language?

Choose Korean or English under App language in Settings. Changing the language preserves workout records and the names of exercises you created.
